    02 · Quick audit

    Three quick wins we already spotted.

    Found in ten minutes of looking at your flyer, menus and listings. Each one quietly costs you visits.

    Win 01

    Two different sets of hours are out there.

    The flyer says Mon–Fri 8–8, Sat 9–10, Sun 11–6. Press listings say Mon–Thu 7–8, Fri–Sat 7–11, Sun 12–6. Mismatched hours cost trust with Google and with anyone who shows up to a locked door.

    → One source of truth: site, Google, Yelp, Apple Maps.
    Win 02

    Two different handles.

    The flyer prints @cafenexus, but the Instagram profile lives at @cafe.nexus. Every mismatch leaks followers who search the printed one.

    → One handle everywhere, linked from Google.
    Win 03

    A menu search engines can barely read.

    Items and prices locked inside images are hard for Google and screen readers to pick up, so searches like “crêpes Worcester” pass you by.

    → Real-text menu + structured data (already in this demo).
